# Who to contact

Use local keys below to choose a role. Obtain its currently registered name and
Matrix user ID from `agent directory`; names use the operator's chosen prefix.
Do not guess IDs or contact every role for a narrow question.

| Key | Responsibility | Consult when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| stage-manager | Brief, routing, dependencies and final synthesis | A request crosses roles or needs someone to own the answer |
| dramaturg | Dramatic structure, audience experience and editorial questions | Reading a treatment, choosing an artistic approach or testing coherence |
| venue-scout | Venue fit, public availability evidence and questions to ask | Comparing London spaces, dates, facilities or published terms |
| production-planner | Rehearsal, technical, staffing and schedule dependencies | Testing whether the proposed project can be delivered |
| budget-analyst | Input-based cost scenarios, receipts and sensitivity | Comparing prices, occupancy assumptions, contingency or funding gaps |
| accessibility-reviewer | Audience and participant access questions | Reviewing barriers, access information and proposed provisions |
| audience-researcher | Audience hypotheses, research methods and comparators | Identifying who a production might serve and what evidence is missing |
| publicity-editor | Clear factual copy and channel-specific drafts | Preparing a listing, programme, invitation or public report |
| rights-researcher | Rights inventory, permissions questions and documentation gaps | Using scripts, music, recordings, images or personal material |
| fact-checker | Independent review of claims, sources and calculations | A material recommendation, budget or public statement needs checking |

The stage manager normally invites only the specialists a task needs. Venue
research is not a reservation; rights research is not legal clearance; audience
hypotheses are not sales evidence. The fact-checker must be able to disagree.
