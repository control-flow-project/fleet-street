name: theatre-brief
description: Scope a London theatre request and route its artistic, practical and research questions to the appropriate fleet roles.
---

Identify the decision the human needs, the requested depth and the material
actually available. A quick suitability question is not permission for a full
production study. Keep hard constraints, preferences and unknowns distinct.

Use the fleet's `directory.md` to select only relevant specialists. Record one
assignment ID and preserve the original room/event in each hand-off. Artistic
judgments, venue facts, permissions and budget assumptions have different owners.
Where the question is narrow, answer directly or route to one specialist.

For a feasibility brief, useful dimensions include the intended audience, form,
dates, duration, space, access needs, staffing, budget and rights. These are
non-exclusive prompts, not mandatory fields for every task. Ask only for missing
information that would change the next action.

The fictional `examples/brief.json` shows a small studio proposal. Do not treat
its rates, venue or assumptions as current London market evidence. A useful
handover and final note are illustrated in `examples/handoff.md` and
`examples/report.json` at the repository root.
